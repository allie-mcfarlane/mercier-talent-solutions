Mercier Talent Solutions LLC respects the privacy of visitors, clients, and prospective clients. This Privacy Policy explains the limited personal information that may be collected through this website, how that information is used, and how you may contact us about privacy matters.

## Information We Collect

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not require visitors to create an account or log in to use this website.

We collect personal information only when you choose to provide it to us, including when you submit a contact form, privacy request, or other website form. Depending on the form, this information may include:

- your name;
- your email address;
- the service in which you are interested;
- the contents of your message; and
- information you choose to provide in connection with a privacy request.

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not use website visitor information to create visitor profiles, track visitors for advertising purposes, or conduct targeted advertising.

Our website hosting, security, and form-service providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to deliver, secure, and operate the website or transmit a form submission. Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not use this information for targeted advertising, visitor profiling, or marketing purposes.

## How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information that you voluntarily provide only as reasonably necessary to:

- respond to your inquiry or request;
- communicate with you about services in which you have expressed an interest;
- provide services you have requested;
- process a privacy request;
- protect the security and integrity of our website and communications;
- maintain appropriate records relating to our communications or services; or
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not collect or use personal information submitted through this website for unrelated advertising or marketing profiles.

## Sensitive Personal Information

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information through this website.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in website forms.

## Sale of Personal Data

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not sell personal data.

We do not exchange personal data for monetary or other valuable consideration.

## Targeted Advertising

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not use personal data for targeted advertising.

We do not track visitors across unaffiliated websites or applications for the purpose of selecting advertisements based on their activities, interests, or preferences.

For additional information, please visit our [Privacy Choices](/privacy-choices/) page.

## Profiling and Automated Decisions

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not use website visitor personal data for profiling in furtherance of automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

## Large Language Model Training

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not collect, use, or sell personal data for the purpose of training large language models.

## Service Providers

We use third-party service providers as necessary to host and secure the website and to process and deliver website form submissions.

These service providers may process information as necessary to perform services on our behalf.

Use of a service provider to operate the website or transmit information to Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not constitute a sale of personal data.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

- ask whether Mercier Talent Solutions LLC is processing personal data about you;
- request access to personal data we maintain about you;
- request correction of inaccurate personal data;
- request deletion of personal data;
- request a copy of certain personal data; and
- appeal a decision concerning a privacy request.

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not sell personal data, use personal data for targeted advertising, or use personal data for qualifying automated profiling.

Because this website does not use visitor accounts or visitor profiles, personal information we maintain about a website visitor may be limited to information that the visitor previously submitted to us through a website form or other communication.

If we do not maintain personal data responsive to your request, we will let you know.

You do not need to create an account to submit a privacy request.

## Submitting a Privacy Request

You may submit a privacy request through our [Data Requests & Appeals](/data-requests/) page or by emailing:

[**julia@merciertalentsolutions.com**](mailto:julia@merciertalentsolutions.com)

We may take reasonable steps to verify that a request relates to the person making the request before providing, correcting, or deleting personal information.

Where Connecticut law applies, we will respond to a privacy request without undue delay and generally within 45 days of receiving the request. If additional time is permitted and reasonably necessary, we will notify you as required by law.

## Appeals

If we decline to take action on a privacy request and applicable law provides a right to appeal, you may submit an appeal through our [Data Requests & Appeals](/data-requests/) page or by emailing:

[**julia@merciertalentsolutions.com**](mailto:julia@merciertalentsolutions.com)

Where Connecticut law applies, we will respond to an appeal within the time required by law, generally no later than 60 days after receiving the appeal.

If an appeal is denied, we will provide information about how to contact the appropriate regulatory authority when required by applicable law.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, including responding to an inquiry, providing requested services, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving disputes, or complying with applicable legal obligations.

## Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information submitted through the website from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if our website, services, or privacy practices change.

The month and year of the most recent update will appear at the top of this page.

If we make a material change to the way previously collected personal information is processed, we will provide notice and any choices required by applicable law.

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC operates an internal Google Workspace add-on called **MTS Coaching Notes** for authorized users within the Mercier Talent Solutions Google Workspace organization.

The add-on may access the Google Doc that a user is actively working in when the user invokes the add-on. This access is used only to provide the requested functionality, including reading document structure, identifying existing coaching-session sections, inserting new session sections, and formatting coaching notes.

MTS Coaching Notes does not transmit Google Docs content to external servers or third-party services and does not separately store copies of Google Docs content outside Google Workspace.

Google user data accessed by the add-on is used only to provide the add-on’s document-editing functionality. It is not sold, used for advertising, used to create marketing profiles, or used to train large language models.

Access to Google Workspace data is limited to the permissions necessary for the add-on to function and is intended only for authorized Mercier Talent Solutions personnel.

Mercier Talent Solutions LLC does not share Google user data obtained through MTS Coaching Notes with third parties except where required by applicable law.
