Mercier Talent Solutions respects the privacy of visitors, clients, and prospective clients. This Privacy Policy explains the limited personal information that may be collected through this website and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

Mercier Talent Solutions does not require visitors to create an account or log in to use this website.

We collect personal information only when you choose to provide it to us, such as by submitting a contact form, privacy request, or other website form. This information may include your name, email address, service interest, and the contents of your message.

Our website hosting, security, and form-service providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and deliver the website and its forms.

Mercier Talent Solutions does not use website analytics or tracking to create profiles of visitors, target advertising, or collect information for marketing purposes.

## How We Use Information

Information that you voluntarily provide is used only as reasonably necessary to:

- respond to your inquiry or request;
- communicate with you about services you have expressed an interest in;
- process a privacy request; or
- maintain appropriate business records relating to your communication with us.

We do not use personal information submitted through the website to build visitor profiles or for unrelated marketing purposes.

## Sensitive Personal Information

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through website forms.

## Sale of Personal Data, Targeted Advertising, and Profiling

Mercier Talent Solutions does not sell personal data.

Mercier Talent Solutions does not use personal data for targeted advertising.

Mercier Talent Solutions does not profile website visitors using personal data for automated decisions.

These practices are not part of our business model.

## Large Language Model Training

Mercier Talent Solutions does not collect, use, or sell personal data for the purpose of training large language models.

## Service Providers

We use third-party service providers as necessary to operate the website and receive website form submissions. These providers may process limited information as necessary to provide those services.

Mercier Talent Solutions does not authorize these providers to sell personal information submitted through our website or use it on our behalf for targeted advertising.

## Privacy Requests and Appeals

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to ask whether Mercier Talent Solutions maintains personal data about you and to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of that information.

Because this website does not use visitor accounts or profiles, the personal data we maintain about a website visitor may be limited to information that the visitor previously submitted to us. If we do not maintain personal data responsive to your request, we will let you know.

Privacy requests and appeals may be submitted through our **Data Requests & Appeals** page or by emailing [**julia@merciertalentsolutions.com**](mailto:julia@merciertalentsolutions.com).

Where Connecticut law applies, we will respond to privacy requests and appeals within the time required by law.

## Data Retention

Information submitted through the website is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the communication, maintain appropriate business records, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Data Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect personal information submitted to us through the website.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if our website or privacy practices change. The month and year of the most recent update will appear at the top of this page.
