Julia Mercier will co-facilitate a session on “Leveraging Group Coaching for Maximum Impact” at the 2022 Annual Conference for the Professional Development Institute alongside Miriam Benor (Director of Attorney Coaching, Pilsbury Winthrop Shaw Pittman LLP), Kara Dodson (Consultant & Coach, Volta Talent Strategies), and Khara Tusa Kelsch (Head of Coaching and Career Development, Steptoe & Johnson).

During this interactive workshop, the facilitators will discuss key elements of group coaching theory, provide a practical framework for implementing group coaching programs tailored to a firm’s specific needs, and facilitate a group coaching demonstration to cement learning objectives and help participants determine how group coaching can enhance internal professional development and talent management programs. Participants will leave the program with practical and actionable ideas for integrating group coaching with their talent strategy, including how group coaching can support sponsorship programs, lateral integration, career development, lawyer and professional staff training, and lawyer business development training and coaching.

For more information about the Professional Development Institute and the 2022 Conference, visit the conference [website](https://web.cvent.com/event/446a29b7-fb83-43dd-8c75-2fcb0fd27763/websitePage:645d57e4-75eb-4769-b2c0-f201a0bfc6ce).
