Julia Mercier has published a new white paper, _The Coaching Dividend: How Coaching Helps Lateral Partners and Their Teams Bring Out the Best in Each Other_.

The paper explores how law firms can use coaching to strengthen lateral integration, develop talent, and build firm culture. Incoming partners bring years of experience and judgment, while established colleagues contribute their own expertise and approaches. Coaching helps make that exchange deliberate, allowing effective practices to spread across the firm.

Featuring the **J.U.D.G.E. Model™**, the paper offers a practical approach to coaching through everyday supervision, delegation, and feedback, with ideas for incorporating the model into lateral integration, partner retreats, and leadership development programs.
