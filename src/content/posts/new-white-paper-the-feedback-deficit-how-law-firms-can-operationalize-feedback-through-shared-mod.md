The Feedback Deficit explores why clear, timely feedback remains difficult in law firms and the impact this has on development, confidence and team performance. Featuring the A.L.I.G.N. Feedback Model™ and Maturity Model™, the paper offers practical ways to build greater clarity, candor and trust.
