**The October edition of the **[Law Journal Newsletters](https://www.merciertalentsolutions.com/s/Leading-Though-Disruption) features Julia’s recent article titled “Leading Through a Period of Disruption”. The pandemic has brought into focus the need for firms and leaders to be strategic about managing people. Firms working to refine their talent strategy will ensure that their associates and partners are engaged with their work, with clients, and with colleagues, and by doing so will enable their firm to be an employer of choice in this competitive market.
